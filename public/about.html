Connecting Talent with Opportunity

Job Market is a modern, web-based recruitment platform built to simplify job discovery and employer management.

Our Mission

Our core objective is to dismantle the barriers that exist in the job search process. We believe that job seekers should have direct, unfettered access to career openings and status tracker updates, and recruiters should have robust tools to source, screen, and select matching candidates without complex interfaces.

Job Market enables complete role-based management, allowing candidate profile organization, resume handling, and administrator oversight to keep the recruitment space secure, organic, and clean.

Platform Highlights

Designed with premium glassmorphic elements, quick searches, and powerful tools for recruiters, candidates, and admins.

Role-based Dashboards

Tailored portals for Candidates to track applications, Recruiters to publish vacancies, and Admins to moderate users.

Robust Filters

Quick search tools to filter positions by location, category, salary range, and experience qualifications.

Resume Uploads

Directly upload PDF, DOCX, or text resumes to candidate profiles, making applications fast and seamless.
